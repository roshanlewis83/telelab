Talkdesk + Salesforce

Understand how Talkdesk and Salesforce can work together to provide an integrated customer service experience.

What is Talkdesk?

Talkdesk is a cloud-based contact center platform that provides voice, digital channels, routing, reporting and other customer experience capabilities.

Contact center agents can use Talkdesk to handle customer interactions while customer information and business processes can be managed through Salesforce.

What is Salesforce?

Salesforce is a cloud-based CRM platform used to manage customer information, cases, accounts, contacts and customer service workflows.

Salesforce Service Cloud provides service agents with the tools they need to manage customer interactions and support cases.

How Talkdesk and Salesforce Work Together

Talkdesk can provide the contact center and telephony capabilities while Salesforce provides the CRM and customer service environment.

Customer → Talkdesk → Salesforce → Agent

CTI and Agent Experience

Computer Telephony Integration (CTI) allows telephony functionality to be presented within the agent's Salesforce workspace.

Depending on the integration, an agent may be able to answer calls, make outbound calls, change availability and access customer information without leaving Salesforce.

What Happens When a Customer Calls?

An incoming customer call can pass through several components before reaching an available agent.

Customer Call → Talkdesk → IVR → Queue → Agent

Salesforce can provide the agent with the customer context and related CRM information while the conversation is being handled.

Screen Pop and Customer Context

One of the major benefits of integrating a contact center platform with Salesforce is the ability to connect a customer interaction with CRM information.

For example, an incoming interaction may allow the agent to locate a customer record, contact or case associated with the interaction.

Salesforce Service Cloud Voice

Salesforce Service Cloud Voice brings voice interactions into the Salesforce service experience.

Voice interactions can be associated with Salesforce records and customer service workflows, allowing agents to work with voice and CRM information in the same environment.

Integration Flow

A simplified Talkdesk and Salesforce customer service flow can look like this:

Customer → Talkdesk → Routing → Agent → Salesforce
