Talkdesk + Salesforce

Understand how Talkdesk and Salesforce can work together to provide an integrated customer service experience.

What is Talkdesk?

Talkdesk is a cloud-based contact center platform that provides voice, digital channels, routing, reporting and other customer experience capabilities.

Talkdesk can handle customer interactions while Salesforce provides customer information and CRM capabilities to the service organization.

What is Salesforce?

Salesforce is a cloud-based CRM platform used to manage customer information, accounts, contacts, cases and customer service workflows.

Salesforce Service Cloud provides service agents with the tools required to manage customer interactions and support processes.

CTI and the Agent Experience

Computer Telephony Integration (CTI) connects telephony functionality with the agent's business application.

Depending on the integration, agents may be able to answer calls, make outbound calls, change their availability and access customer information from within the Salesforce environment.

Screen Pop and Customer Context

One benefit of integrating a contact-center platform with Salesforce is the ability to connect customer interactions with CRM information.

Depending on the configuration, an incoming interaction may allow the agent to locate a customer record, contact or case associated with the interaction.

Salesforce Service Cloud Voice

Salesforce Service Cloud Voice brings voice interactions into the Salesforce service experience.

Voice interactions can be associated with Salesforce records and customer-service workflows, allowing agents to work with voice and CRM information in the same environment.

Service Cloud Voice architecture can vary depending on the voice provider and Salesforce configuration.
